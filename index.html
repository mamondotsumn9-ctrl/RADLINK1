Possible live dashboard indicators

Verified professionals, genuine vacancies, applications, placements, time-to-fill, local placements and employer satisfaction. A real dashboard should show methodology and reporting dates.

Responsible data use

Use aggregated data, restrict access, minimise personal information and comply with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Never publish home addresses or unverified allegations.

Accountability by design

Build trust into recruitment

Technology cannot eliminate corruption, but it can improve traceability and reduce opportunities for opaque processes.

✓ Verified identities

Verify facilities, company representatives and relevant professional registration before granting trusted status.

✓ Transparent vacancy criteria

Keep role requirements, deadlines and documented changes visible to authorised users.

✓ Audit trails and human review

Record relevant actions and route reports for fair review. A report is not proof of wrongdoing.

Submit a demo integrity report

This prototype does not send reports to an authority or store them on a server. Do not include names or sensitive personal information.
